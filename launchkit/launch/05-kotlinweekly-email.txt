LaunchKit for Android: ship a paid Android app in days, not months
https://ahaariyan.github.io/presell/launchkit/?utm_source=kotlinweekly&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=presell-sep2026

It's a Jetpack Compose codebase on Google's recommended app architecture, laid out like Now in Android, with Play Billing, paywall, onboarding, settings, sign-in, analytics and CI already built. The part Kotlin readers may find interesting: custom Android Lint rules that fail the build on layer violations, and an AGENTS.md per module so AI coding agents respect the module boundaries. It's a pre-order (v1 ships 1 October) with a public spec sheet and module map on the page.
