Questions

Why not Google's "Now in Android" sample? It's free.

It's an excellent architecture showcase and we learned from it. It has no billing, no paywall, no onboarding, no store-compliance pieces — the parts that take a solo developer the longest and that every paid app needs.

Does it lock me into Firebase or any service?

No. Analytics, crash reporting and sign-in sit behind interfaces with a no-op implementation. Delete what you don't want.

What do I actually receive?

An invitation to a private GitHub repository (template you can "Use this template" from), a docs site with step-by-step setup for Play Console products, and updates as libraries move.

Can I use it for client work?

Yes — unlimited apps, yours or clients'. You may not resell the kit itself.

Native Android only, on purpose. KMP is on the list if buyers ask for it.
